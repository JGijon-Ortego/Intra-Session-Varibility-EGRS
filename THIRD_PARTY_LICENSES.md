## Brain Connectivity Toolbox (BCT)

The following files are derived from, equivalent to, or depend on the Brain Connectivity Toolbox ([https://sites.google.com/site/bctnet](https://sites.google.com/site/bctnet)):

- 'Graph/computeClusteringCoefficient.m': derived from the BCT clustering coefficient functions.
- 'Graph/computePathLength.m': depends on 'distance_wei' and 'charpath' from the BCT.
- 'Graph/computeNodeStrength.m': equivalent to 'strengths_und' from the BCT.

Reference: Rubinov M, Sporns O (2010). Complex network measures of brain connectivity: uses and interpretations. NeuroImage 52:1059-1069.
